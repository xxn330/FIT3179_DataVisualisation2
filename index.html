08 / ACQUISITION HISTORY

How long after creation was art acquired?

Some artworks enter collections shortly after creation. Others arrive many decades later.

Creation-to-Acquisition Lag by Department

The central line represents the median. Boxes contain the middle half of values.

Key insight: Contemporary Asian Art has a one-year median lag. International Art has a median of 53 years. This does not measure gallery efficiency.

Source: QAGOMA Collection dataset. Based on 12,173 suitably dated records. Approximate dates were excluded. Individual outlier points are hidden.

Together, these findings highlight how cultural heritage is collected, represented and recorded.

12 / METHODOLOGY

Understanding the data.

This exhibition explores recorded artwork categories, creation periods, locations and acquisition practices to identify patterns across collections.

Different charts and maps use position, length, area and colour to show comparisons, distributions and relationships. Filters and tooltips support further exploration.

The datasets cover different collections and regions. Missing dates, classification differences and inferred acquisition routes may affect comparisons. The results do not represent all artworks in Australia.
