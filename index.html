Exploring Australia's visual arts heritage through public art collections.

01 / INTRODUCTION

More than art. A record of culture.

Australian public galleries preserve artworks from different cultures and historical periods. Outdoor art also shapes Australian cities.

This story explores QAGOMA's collection, alongside public art from Brisbane, Melbourne and Canberra.

About This Visualisation

Who: Visitors, students and Australian art enthusiasts.

What: Artwork categories, dates, origins, locations and acquisition records.

Why: To understand patterns across public art collections.

How: Interactive charts and geographic maps. Colours, positions and sizes represent data values. Hover to explore additional details.

11 / METHODOLOGY

Understanding the data

These visualisations describe recorded collections. They do not represent all Australian artworks.

Dates, classifications and geographic coverage vary. Some records contain missing or ambiguous information. Comparisons should be interpreted within these limitations.

Data sources include QAGOMA, Brisbane City Council, City of Melbourne and ACT Government.
